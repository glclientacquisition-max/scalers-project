# No AI slop eval

After a rewrite, pass or fail each check. Fix failures before returning.

Detect requests: each pattern named, line quoted, short fix. No rewrite. No authorship guess.

## Principles

1. User point unchanged. No invented claims, examples, stats, or opinions.
2. Distinctive voice kept (blunt Kenya-desk nouns stay blunt).
3. Strong human sentences left alone.
4. Cutting matches the slop. No golfed one-liners that strip meaning.
5. Desk strings stay utilitarian. No new subtitle under a heading.
6. Generic sentences fail the portability test or get a Scalers-specific fact.
7. Active voice with a human subject where the draft supports it.

## Words and patterns

1. Banned vocabulary and empty adverbs gone unless quoted as a bad example.
2. Binary contrasts, throat-clearing, faux insight, colon reveals, dramatic fragments gone.
3. Weasel attribution named or cut.
4. No em dash or en dash in UI-facing strings.
5. No emoji headings. No decorative bold mid-sentence.
6. No fake-profound kicker. End on the next action or the last concrete fact.

## Final

1. Writer would still recognize the draft.
2. Edit output includes the full draft and What changed.
3. Detect output does not score or claim AI authorship.
