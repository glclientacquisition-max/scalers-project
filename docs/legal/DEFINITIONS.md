# Shared definitions

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Not yet in force. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

Every legal document in this folder uses these words the same way.

- **Scalers**, **we**, **us**: [Registered company name], a company registered in Kenya, [P.O. Box / physical address].
- **Business** or **you**: the company or person who signs up and uses Scalers for their business.
- **Owner**: the person who controls the Business's account and the Desk.
- **User**: anyone the Business lets into its Desk account.
- **Caller**: a person who phones a Business number answered by Scalers.
- **Assistant**: the AI voice Business Assistant that answers calls for the Business.
- **Desk**: the Scalers web app where the Business sees calls, requests and settings.
- **Service**: the Assistant, the Desk, notifications and anything else we provide.
- **Business Data**: facts the Business gives us about itself (name, services, prices, hours, coverage areas, catalogue).
- **Caller Data**: personal data about Callers, such as phone number, name, what they said, transcripts, recordings, requests and bookings.
- **Package**: a plan with a set number of call minutes. **On-demand minutes** are extra minutes beyond a Package.
- **Private Beta**: the current early stage of the Service (see Beta Terms).
- **Subprocessor**: another company we use to run the Service that may handle Caller Data.
- **Data Protection Act**: Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 and its regulations.
- **ODPC**: the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.
