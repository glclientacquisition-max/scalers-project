# Acceptable Use and Private Beta Terms

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Not yet in force. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

## In short
Use Scalers only for lawful business calls. Do not use it to mislead, harass or spam people, or for emergencies. The AI can make mistakes, so you must confirm your business facts and check requests before acting. Scalers is in Private Beta: features, prices and limits can change, things may break, and charging may be off for now. These terms are part of the [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

Words in **bold** are defined in [DEFINITIONS.md](DEFINITIONS.md).

## Part A: Acceptable use

### 1. Do
- Use the Service for your real, lawful business.
- Give the Assistant true facts and keep them current.
- Tell Callers about AI answering and recording.
- Keep your Desk login private.

### 2. Do not
- Break any law, including the Data Protection Act, the Consumer Protection Act, 2012, and the Computer Misuse and Cybercrimes Act, 2018.
- Use the Assistant to mislead Callers, for example about prices, or to pretend it is a human when asked.
- Send spam, or harass, threaten or abuse anyone.
- Use the Service for illegal goods or services, gambling, adult content, or [other restricted categories].
- Try to hack, overload, copy or reverse-engineer the Service, or get into other Businesses' data.
- Resell the Service without our written permission.

### 3. Not for emergencies
The Assistant cannot call emergency services or transfer a call to a person. Do not rely on it for medical, security or other emergencies.

### 4. AI limits
- The AI can mishear names, numbers, times or places, and can give wrong answers.
- It only knows the facts you confirm. It must not give medical, legal or financial advice.
- Check every request and booking before acting on it.

### 5. If rules are broken
We may suspend the account, remove content, or end the Service, and report unlawful activity to the authorities.
