# Privacy Policy

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Not yet in force. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

## In short
This policy explains what personal data Scalers collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019. For calls to a business, that business decides how Caller Data is used and we handle it for them. For our own customers (Owners and Users), we decide. Some of our providers are outside Kenya, and we use safeguards for that. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

Words in **bold** are defined in [DEFINITIONS.md](DEFINITIONS.md).

- **For Owners and Users:** Scalers is the data controller of your account data.

## 2. What we collect
**From Callers (for the Business):**
- Phone number, and name if you give it
- What you say on the call: audio recording, transcript, and the request or booking you make
- Technical call records: time, length, and call traces (how the Assistant handled each turn)

**From Owners and Users:**
- Name, phone number, email, login details
- Business Data: business name, services, prices, hours, coverage areas, catalogue
- How you use the Desk, and billing and usage records

## 3. Why we use it
| Purpose | Legal basis |
| --- | --- |
| Answer calls and record requests for the Business | Business's instructions; the Business's lawful basis (contract or legitimate interest) |
| Notify the Owner about calls (SMS, WhatsApp, email) | Contract with the Business |
| Run, secure and fix the Service | Contract; legitimate interest |
| Check and improve call quality | Legitimate interest, using call traces kept only 30 days |
| Bill Packages and minutes | Contract; legal obligation (tax records) |

We do **not** sell personal data. We do not use Caller Data for our own marketing.

## 4. Automated processing
The Assistant uses AI to understand Callers and reply. It does not make decisions with legal or similarly major effects on Callers. The Business reviews requests and decides what to do.

## 5. Who we share it with
Only with the providers we need to run the Service (our **Subprocessors**), listed in the [Data Processing Agreement](DATA_PROCESSING_AGREEMENT.md#subprocessors): telephony, speech, AI, hosting, database, SMS, WhatsApp and email. We may also share data if the law or a court requires it.

## 8. Security
We use access controls, encrypted connections, and separate data per Business. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach is likely to put people at risk, we will tell the affected Business without delay, and the ODPC within 72 hours as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights
Under the Data Protection Act you can:
- be told how your data is used
- see a copy of your data
- correct wrong data
- ask us to delete data, or object to its use
- move your data, where the law allows
- complain to the ODPC (www.odpc.go.ke)

Callers: please contact the Business first. Owners and Users: email [contact email, e.g. privacy@scalers.co.ke]. We reply within [time, e.g. 7 days / as the law requires].
