# Call Recording and AI Notice

> **DRAFT for legal review.** Not yet in force. Last updated: 9 October 2026.

## In short
Callers should know they are speaking to an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded. This page gives a short spoken line for the start of each call, in English and Swahili, and a longer written notice for websites and the Desk. **The live greeting has not been changed.** The Voice and Brain teams must add this line to the greeting in a later pull request.

## 1. Spoken line (for the greeting)
Keep it short so callers are not kept waiting.

**English:**
> "Hi, you've reached [Business name]. I'm an AI assistant, and this call may be recorded to help us serve you."

**Swahili:**
> "Habari, umepiga simu kwa [Business name]. Mimi ni msaidizi wa AI, na simu hii inaweza kurekodiwa ili tukuhudumie vyema."

Optional extra line if a caller asks:
- EN: "Your details go to [Business name] so they can follow up. You can ask them to delete them."
- SW: "Taarifa zako zinatumwa kwa [Business name] ili wakufuatilie. Unaweza kuwaomba wazifute."

## 2. Written notice (for websites, the Desk and business listings)
> **Calls to this number are answered by an AI assistant.**
> When you call [Business name], an AI assistant provided by Scalers may answer. It can mishear or make mistakes, so [Business name] checks every request. The call may be recorded and transcribed. Your number, name and what you ask for are shared with [Business name] so they can help you. [Business name] decides how your information is used, and Scalers handles it for them. To see, correct or delete your information, contact [Business name] or email [contact email, e.g. privacy@scalers.co.ke]. More: [Privacy Policy link].
>
> **This line is not for emergencies.** For emergencies call 999, 112 or 911.

## 3. Rules for wiring it (for Voice and Brain)
- Play the line at the start of every call, before any data is collected.
- Choose English or Swahili from the Business's language setting. [Decide: play both, or one?]
- Do not lengthen dead air at the greeting (see #645). Measure greeting latency after the change.
- Owners must not be able to switch the notice off.
- Test on staging first.
